# Weekly challenge: a possible new feature

_Sharon · Nov 2, 2026. Research on adding real stakes to Run Money. Public sources, read Nov 2 unless another date is shown._

## Why

- Skipping a week costs little: about $0.96 on $1,000, and about $0.11 on the average ~$116 deposit (Aave ~5%, Oct 20–Nov 1; 18 depositors, ~$2.1K, Oct 12).
- StepBet and Renno make skipping matter. You pay an entry. Hit your goal and you get it back plus a share of what others missed. Miss it and you lose your entry.

## The gap

- Dollars, no token: [StepBet](https://www.stepbet.com/) (steps; $148.9M paid out by its own count, Oct 16), [Renno](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/renno/id6781789465) (runs; launched Sep 2), [Stride](https://joinstride.app/) and [RunMatch](https://runmatch.io/get-paid-to-run) (runs).
- Crypto, with their own token: [Moonwalk](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/moonwalk-fitness) (MF −96.5%), [STEPN Marathon](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/stepn) (GMT −99.8%), [Runwago](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/runwago/) (−99.93%), Higher Athletics (HIGHER −99.9%, Oct 16).
- Someone tried USDC with no token: Gauntlet, where friends bet USDC on Strava challenges on Base. It launched Apr 8, and the site didn't load on Oct 21 or Nov 2 ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/gauntlet-2), [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47032040)).
- I couldn't find a live USDC, no-token version. I checked the US App Store, DefiLlama, GitHub, Farcaster and the web, but not Google Play, X or Telegram.

## The feature

1. Optional, on top of savings. Deposits aren't touched.
2. One pool with one fixed USDC entry. $5 is a placeholder; rivals run about $5–40.
3. Run 5K and you get your entry back plus a share of missed entries. Miss and you lose your entry.
4. Always on. It works with two people, so there's nothing to schedule.
5. If Strava fails, the week is void and entries go back.
6. To decide: if everyone misses, roll the money over or refund it.

Why one pool: with few members, different amounts would split people into pools that never fill. Later, bigger entries could go in the same pool for a bigger share, the way deposits work now.

Example, not a forecast: 18 people enter at $5 and half miss. That's $45 shared by 9 runners, or $5 each.

## How it could earn

- [Renno](https://rennoapp.com/rules): 15% of missed money (0% at launch, 15% from Sep 24), plus Renno+ at $6.99/month.
- [StepBet](https://www.stepbet.com/faq): 15% of the pot, plus memberships from $10.99/month (Oct 13).
- For us: start at 0%, add a cut later, then subscriptions.
- Size check, not a forecast: at $5 with half missing and a 15% cut, about $0.38 per person a week. 100 people is about $1,950 a year, and 1,000 is about $19,500.

## What it comes with

- Legal: needs a lawyer first. Rivals argue that effort decides the result, not chance, and I found no ruling either way. Gambling rules differ by US state and by European country, and crypto adds licensing questions.
- Few users: a pool needs people, which is why it starts as one pool.
- Strava: a failed sync can't cost someone their entry.
- Reconnecting still matters: historically 8–10 athletes hit 5K most rounds on these same stakes, and it's closer to 3–4 now while athletes reconnect.
- Reach: crypto users for now. A runner with a card will pick Renno or Stride.
