# SEO keywords: words to use

_Issue #2 · Nov 2, 2026 · Sharon. What people type into Google, and how to use it in posts, captions and bios. From Google autocomplete and Trends, which show relative interest, not search volumes._

**Mike, one question:** do walks count toward the 5K? I'm assuming yes, since pace isn't checked. If so, "get paid to walk" opens up.

## Runners (IG, Strava)

On US Trends, "get paid to run" and "get paid to walk" each get about 3 to 4 times the interest of "stepbet" (some of that is errands and ads).

| People type | Line to use |
|---|---|
| get paid to run | Get paid to run. Hit your 5K and earn your interest, plus a share from members who skipped. |
| get paid to walk (if walks count) | Get paid to walk. 5K a week, any pace. |
| run 5k a week | Run 5K a week. Your savings earn more. |
| run 5k once a week | One 5K a week. That's the whole goal. |
| is running 5k a week enough | Is 5K a week enough? Enough to keep your interest. |
| running accountability | Skip your 5K and that week's interest goes to the people who ran. |
| apps like stepbet | Like StepBet, but a missed week costs that week's interest, not your deposit. |
| strava rewards | Already on Strava? Your runs count. |

## Crypto (X)

| People type | Line to use |
|---|---|
| run to earn, move to earn | Run to earn, no token. Rewards are interest from Aave. |
| sweat token ending | SWEAT ends in its current form on Dec 30. Here's run to earn with no token. |
| stepn alternative | Looking past STEPN? No token here, just interest from Aave on Base. |
| aave usdc yield on base | Aave USDC on Base: [X]%. Members who hit their 5K: [Y]%. Week of [dates]. |
| no loss lottery | Like a no-loss lottery, minus the luck. Hit your 5K and you earn. |

Any rate or $ amount needs a date and a source. The membership is always "one-time".

## Don't use

- **5km:** "run 5k" gets about 5 times the interest worldwide, and "run 5km" barely registers in the US.
- **5k a week, on its own:** those are salary searches. Always add run or running.
- **"app":** there's no app, so say "get paid to run", not "apps that pay you to run".
- **make money running:** mostly errands and ads.
- **no-loss savings:** nobody searches it. Fine as a description, not as a keyword.

## FAQ additions

Answers need your OK.

1. **Is there a Run Money app?** No. Just the website, runmoney.app, on desktop.
2. **Can you really get paid to run?** Yes. Hit your 5K each week and you earn your interest, plus a share from members who missed.
3. **Do I have to run the 5K all at once?** No, it can add up across runs.
4. **Is this like betting apps?** No. Miss a week and you lose that week's interest, not your deposit.
5. **Is this a no-loss lottery?** No draw and no luck. You hit a weekly goal, and the membership is a one-time fee.
6. **Is Aave safe?** Point to the Risks page.

Searching "run money" brings up games and step apps, on Google and the App Store, so always write runmoney.app in full.
