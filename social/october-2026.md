## From Oct 19: the weekly rhythm

| Day | Post | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Weekly rate post: usual format (net rate, USDC/ETH split, vs Aave alone) + one plain line for runners | Regular |
| Wed | Rotating: how it works / a real member question / vs a bank or Coinbase rate | Regular |
| Thu | Thread | Experiment |
| Sat | Meme, timed to weekend long runs or race weekends | Experiment |

**Regular posts** keep the account looking active and trustworthy. Same slot every week so people know what to expect.

**Experiments** are for reach. Threads and memes are the formats most likely to get replies and reposts from people who don't follow us yet. Each week we compare them against the regular posts and the 40–90 impressions a post gets today. Whatever works, we do more of in November.
