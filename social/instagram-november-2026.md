# Instagram plan: November 2026

_Plan, Oct 30, 2026 · Sharon. Living plan; will edit as we go._

Run Money's first month on Instagram. It starts Mon Nov 2 with 0 followers. IG is for runners, in plain words; X stays the crypto channel. Signups still come mostly from creators and DMs, and IG is where people check us out first, so it has to look alive.

## Goals

1. First followers, and an account that looks active.
2. Find the posts non-followers share. IG shows Reels to people who don't follow you, and posts people send to a friend get the most reach. So the main bet is runner memes as Reels.
3. Sell the habit, not the money. At ~$2.1k deposited (Oct 2026), weekly rewards are small.

## Weeks 3–4 (Nov 16–29): once I have the login

Without the login, I keep posting 3 a week through Buffer. With it:
1. 3 posts a week: 2 fun (memes, race-weekend jokes), 1 explainer.
2. 15 minutes a day following and commenting on run clubs, races, run creators and running meme accounts. Reply to every comment within the hour.
3. Stories, once there are followers to see them: share each post, a weekly poll ("Hit your 5 km this week?"), and a Monday "last week in dollars" with that week's real, dated numbers.
4. Scam filter: Hidden Words, an IG setting that hides comments and message requests with phrases we pick ("DM me", WhatsApp links). Crypto accounts attract scam bots.
5. First creator collab, if the profile looks ready. A Collab post shows on both accounts. If the creator gets a free membership, the post is labelled "Paid partnership". Check where their audience is first: in the UK, crypto promotions (memes too) need FCA approval.

## Post rules

1. Normal Reels, not Trial reels. A Trial reel only shows to non-followers and stays off the profile. At 0 followers a normal Reel reaches the same people and fills the profile.
2. Our own text on every reused clip. Since Apr 2026, IG stops recommending straight reposts.
3. Captions use words runners search ("5k a week", "running motivation"). IG posts now show up in Google.
4. No rates in the feed; rates stay on X.
5. Up to 5 hashtags (IG's limit): #5k, #runningcommunity, race tags.
6. Wording:
   - "savings", "deposit", "digital dollars (USDC)", not stake, yield or APY
   - "week", not epoch
   - "one-time membership", no amount until the new fee is set
   - "Miss a week? Lose that week's interest, not your deposit." Never "guaranteed" or "no risk".
   - "Built on Aave", "Runs on Strava": nothing that sounds like either one backs us
   - "Saturday 5K", not "parkrun" (it's a trademark)
